OUR PRINCIPLES

How We Build Software

Simple, thoughtful design guided by safety, clarity, and respect for users.

SIMPLICITY FIRST

Simple is Always Better

We believe the best solutions are the simplest ones. We avoid unnecessary complexity, use lightweight tools, and focus on what really helps users. Clean, straightforward code is faster to run, easier to read, and more reliable over time.

Our Standard: Direct solutions, quick tests, and zero confusing clutter.
SAFETY & TRUST

Humans Always in Charge

  • 1. Everyday Checks: AI helpers check system health, run tests, and organize details automatically.
  • 2. Team Organization: President Komi plans tasks, creates backups, and prepares work locally.
  • 3. Final Approval: Important actions like publishing updates or changing sensitive data require Founder Soya's direct permission.
